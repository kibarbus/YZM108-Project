Frequently Asked Questions

How does the subscription system work?

UnBoxed Pages works on a monthly subscription basis. When you subscribe, our club carefully selects and packages themed books along with exclusive university-themed surprises, delivering them straight to your door at Kocaeli University every month.

Can I choose or suggest books for the upcoming months?

Yes, absolutely! At the end of each month during our live book analysis sessions, active members can drop suggestions for future themes and specific book titles. We love keeping our community involved in the curation process.

When are the live book discussions held?

Live digital discussion and analysis sessions are held during the final week of every month. The access links and exact schedules are shared directly with subscribers via email.
