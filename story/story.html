Our Story

Developed for the Kocaeli University Book Club, UnBoxed Pages was born out of a desire to bridge the gap between traditional reading habits and the digital ecosystem. We believe that reading shouldn't be a solitary chore, but rather an engaging, interactive, and community-driven social experience.

Our journey started with a simple question: How can we make reading more exciting and consistent for university students? The answer was to create a structured monthly ritual. By curating specific themes and combining them with physical, tangible book boxes, we turn a standard hobby into an event everyone looks forward to.

"Combining the traditional reading experience with a modern subscription system."

Today, UnBoxed Pages is more than just a delivery service. At the end of each month, our members join live book discussion and analysis sessions through digital links shared with them. While continuing the cultural interaction within the university in a digital environment, we turn reading into a dynamic social activity.
